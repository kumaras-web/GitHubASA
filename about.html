OUR STORY

Rooted in purpose,
driven by craft.

Aravind Associates began with a simple conviction: that thoughtful architecture can quietly improve everyday life. What started as a small practice has grown into a collaborative studio of architects, engineers, and designers who share a common approach — listen carefully, design deliberately, and build with restraint.

Over the years, our work has spanned residential, commercial, institutional, and civic projects. Each commission — whether a private home or a public building — is treated with the same rigour: understand the site, understand the people, and let the design emerge from both.
